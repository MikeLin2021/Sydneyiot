Selected impact

Rapid research translation at a hospital entrance.

The COVID-19 eGate brought QR-based screening, temperature sensing and near-real-time data analytics into one operational pathway at The Children’s Hospital at Westmead.

“Australian-first” integrated entry screening system, trialled by more than 1,500 staff and regular visitors over eight months.
